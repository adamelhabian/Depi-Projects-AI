# Visual assets

## Current blue/apricot photography

`educator-blue.webp` and `materials-blue.webp` are the active EduFlow photographs. Both were generated with the built-in image generation tool (not CLI/API fallback), then converted without cropping to WebP for delivery. Original generated PNGs are kept as `educator-blue.png` and `materials-blue.png`. The prior green library photograph below is retained as a legacy asset and is no longer referenced by the interface or brand guide.

These are illustrative scenes, not real customers, testimonials, document previews, or evidence of product use. Blue, navy, apricot and cream tie them to the current identity; plants, green clothing and green props were excluded deliberately.

## Brand-guide portrait

`overview-teacher.webp` is an existing local illustration of a male teacher preparing a lesson. The brand guide uses it once in its identity board and the export script copies it into `public/brand/`; it is not a customer photo, testimonial, or product-use evidence. Its original image-generation/source details are not recorded in this repository.

### Educator prompt

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: hero photograph for EduFlow AI, an Egyptian learning-center management and teacher preparation website. Create a premium editorial landscape photograph, 3:2 or wide landscape. An Egyptian woman teacher in her early thirties wearing a modest powder-blue blouse, dark navy trousers, preparing a lesson at a light oak desk in a bright contemporary learning-center studio. A cream notebook with blank pages, apricot pencil case, silver laptop, a small stack of blue and cream books. Natural warm daylight, restrained navy / learning blue / apricot / ivory palette, light blue wall and soft cream curtains. Candid focused expression, authentic skin and hands, professional but approachable. Teacher occupies right half with spacious softly blurred left half. Palette must have NO green: no plants, no foliage, no green clothing, no green objects, no green shelves. No text, brand logos, signage, watermark, robot, holograms or interface overlays. Natural editorial photography, refined quiet composition, not stock corporate handshake.

### Materials prompt

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: contextual image for EduFlow AI lesson preparation and learning materials cards. Editorial still-life landscape photograph of a teaching preparation desk, viewed at a gentle overhead angle: neatly stacked cobalt blue, navy and cream exercise books, open ivory notebook with very faint unlettered geometry diagrams, apricot pencil case, blue pencil, small brushed silver paperclips and a closed powder-blue folder. Composition with tactile paper edges, clear space and a human hand gently arranging the folder at the edge. Warm natural daylight, soft blue background, thoughtful realistic textures and quiet premium educational editorial art direction. Restrained #315DA8 blue, #F2B880 apricot, #14243B navy and ivory palette. No green anywhere, no plants, no foliage. No readable words, no watermarks, no logos, no UI, no neon.

A young Egyptian university woman with dark brown hair in a loose bun, olive complexion, wearing a sage green knit sweater and small over-ear cream headphones, working attentively with an open notebook and a silver laptop at a wooden desk in a beautiful quiet sunlit university library. Medium shot, natural candid profile, subject occupies right half, generous blurred deep forest-green library shelving and plants on left. Warm afternoon window light, analog photographic grain, authentic human skin and textures, sophisticated Kinfolk magazine photography, muted warm greens and oat beige, relaxed confident mood, dark enough left side for a cream text overlay.
