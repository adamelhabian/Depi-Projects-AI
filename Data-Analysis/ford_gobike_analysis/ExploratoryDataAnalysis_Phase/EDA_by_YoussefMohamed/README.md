## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end exploratory data analysis (EDA) of the **Ford GoBike** bike-sharing system (San Francisco Bay area). The investigation covers user demographics, ride duration distributions, peak commute behaviors, spatial station flow, and statistical contrasts between subscription tiers.

---

## 📊 Key Empirical Findings

1. **Commuter Backbone**:
   - **89.2% of all trips** are made by annual **Subscribers**, demonstrating that the system serves primarily as a daily commute utility rather than a casual leisure service.
2. **The Duration Paradox**:
   - Casual **Customers average 21.9 minutes** per trip—more than **double the 10.7 minutes** averaged by Subscribers. Customers utilize bikes for leisure and exploration, while Subscribers prioritize rapid point-to-point transit.
3. **Core Demographic**:
   - Ridership is heavily concentrated among young working professionals aged **25 to 35 years** (median age: **32 years**).
4. **Gender Disparity**:
   - **Male cyclists represent 71.2%** of trips, Female cyclists represent **22.3%**, and Other members make up **6.5%**, presenting a significant demographic expansion opportunity.
5. **High Fleet Turnover**:
   - **97.4% of all rides conclude in under 30 minutes**, proving the effectiveness of system pricing structures in maintaining continuous fleet circulation.
6. **Transit Arteries**:
   - The top origin stations connect directly to major regional transit hubs (**Market St at 10th St**, **San Francisco Caltrain Station 2**, and **Powell St BART Station**).

---

## 🚀 Strategic Business Recommendations

- **Tiered Leisure Memberships**: Introduce a "Weekend Explorer Pass" or flexible multi-hour pass targeted at recreational Customers to convert high-duration rides into recurring subscriptions.
- **Female Cyclist Acquisition**: Implement well-lit docking stations, step-through e-bikes, and community safety initiatives to address the 3:1 gender divide.
- **Dynamic Fleet Rebalancing**: Prioritize van rebalancing between BART/Caltrain transit hubs and financial/commercial centers during peak morning (7:30–9:30 AM) and evening (4:30–6:30 PM) rush hours.
- **Scale Equity Programs**: Expand community outreach and subsidized enrollment for the "Bike Share for All" program (currently at 9.9% adoption).
