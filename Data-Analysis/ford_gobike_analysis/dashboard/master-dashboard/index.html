About this platform, Data Lineage & Schema Definition

System Lineage: Sourced from San Francisco Bay Area Ford GoBike (now Bay Wheels) trip history system logs. Aggregated from over 174,724 anonymized GPS coordinates, dock telemetry records, and user subscription records spanning San Francisco, East Bay (Oakland/Berkeley), and San Jose directly from Supabase Cloud PostgreSQL.

Methodology: Net flow balance is calculated as $Net = Arrivals - Departures$. Negative net flow indicates a station requiring bike replenishment ("Deficit"), while positive net flow signals docking bay saturation risk ("Surplus").
