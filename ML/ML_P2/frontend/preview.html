Key Drivers of Donor Likelihood

Key predictive signals identified from feature importance evaluation in Project 2

Strongest Driver

Capital Gains & Wealth

Capital gains represent investment returns and disposable wealth. Individuals with positive capital gains have the highest correlation with earning > $50K.

High Influence

Educational Attainment

Holders of Bachelors, Masters, or Doctorate degrees exhibit significantly higher odds of surpassing the $50K threshold compared to basic education levels.

Demographic Signal

Career Seniority & Age

Experienced professionals in their peak earning years (35-55) in executive and managerial occupations represent the primary philanthropic demographic.
